Our Story

Meet Tom Lingard

Owner & Founder

After roles in Customer Service and later providing Director-level PA support in academia, Tom saw first-hand how much time professionals were losing to admin — and knew there had to be a better way. He launched The Remote Assistant in August 2021 with a simple mission: give busy professionals their time back.

What started as a one-person operation has grown into an award-winning team of six, each bringing specialist skills across admin, marketing, and project management. The team now supports 50+ organisations across consultancies and higher education — the two sectors where Tom's experience runs deepest.

The approach is simple: we do not try to be everything to everyone. We go deep in the sectors we know best, we assign named team members to every client, and we embed into your workflow until it feels like we have always been part of your team.
